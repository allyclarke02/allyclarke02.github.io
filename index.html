About me

A little context about who I am and what I do.

I’m a graduate student at the University of Washington, working with Dr. Kat Steele in the Neuromechanics and Mobility Lab. My work focuses on monitoring recovery from orthopedic surgery using wearables and video-based assessments.

Outside of research, I enjoy running, baking, and doing puzzles. I’m always glad to connect with people working on related questions.
